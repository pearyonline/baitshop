A collection of Gon' Fishin's best performing worms!


Slimebo

High durability, low charm. Will outwiggle anything.

Twist & Shout

Twist coils the line, Shout distracts the fish with high-pitched squeals. Unruly, inseparable, mildly effective.

Glo-Wanda

Glows under stress. Fish can't resist her shimmer.

Chucklegrub

Tells jokes, confuses predators. Mostly useless.

Dirt Reynolds

Slides into any bait cup like butter. Suave wriggler.

Sir Squirmalot

Noble, loyal, dramatic flailer. Loves damsels in distress.
